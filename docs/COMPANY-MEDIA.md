# Company media attribution

Company icons are downloaded from the icon links published by the official company websites. `apps/web/lib/company-logos.json` records the original asset URL and website for each checked-in image. Images identify the named employer and do not imply a partnership or endorsement. They are not recolored or presented as FrontlaneJobs branding.

The initial verified assets cover GitLab, Canonical, MongoDB, and CloudSEK. Other employers use initials. If an icon fails to load, the UI falls back to initials. Company descriptions link to the official website and omit unknown size, funding, and team details. No office/team photos are invented.

Review an employer's asset and usage terms before adding new imagery. Keep source attribution with every curated asset. Company metadata is keyed by exact employer source identity; do not merge companies by fuzzy names or infer their website from the ATS hostname.
